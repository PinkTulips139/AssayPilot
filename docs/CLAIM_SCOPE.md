# Claim scope

Allowed: same-study, seen-batch policy evaluation on registered entities and physical plates withheld from development. Development coverage-only AUC 0.91806 ranked first; confirmation T1 0.69018 and T2 0.88917 ranked fourth. Frozen confirmation changed the policy-selection conclusion. QACS multi-objective benefit was not supported in development.

Not supported: unseen-batch or scaffold/chemical-structure generalization; chemical novelty; external-study generalization; wet-lab benefit, drug efficacy or clinical benefit; universal optimality of Diversity or Uncertainty; independent biological plate replication between T1/T2.

T1/T2 are performance-exposed. They cannot independently confirm a new method informed by their outcomes. Bootstrap intervals quantify paired initial-seed uncertainty on fixed entities and plates. They are not biological, cross-batch or population confidence intervals. Higher conditional repeatability cannot replace the frozen primary endpoint.
